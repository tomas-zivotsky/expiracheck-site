# Expiracheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

## 1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Expiracheck handles personal
information when you use the mobile application and its website.

Expiracheck is developed by Tomáš Životský.
For privacy questions or requests, contact:
[zivotsky.t@gmail.com](mailto:zivotsky.t@gmail.com).

## 2. Information handled by the app

### Information you enter

Expiracheck stores information about the items you manage,
including their titles, renewal periods, notification severity,
and expiration dates.

This information is stored locally on your device. If you use
the Google Calendar integration, information needed to create
corresponding events and reminders is also sent to Google Calendar.

### Google Account information

When you connect a Google Account, Expiracheck accesses your
Google Account identifier and email address to identify the
connected account.

Expiracheck receives authorization tokens that allow it to access
Google Calendar with your permission. These tokens are stored
locally on your device. The app does not receive your Google password.

### Google Calendar information

Expiracheck accesses your calendar list so you can choose a calendar
for the integration.

The app accesses and manages event information needed to create,
update, and delete calendar events and reminders associated with
items managed in Expiracheck. This can include event identifiers,
titles, dates, times, and reminder settings.

The calendar event permission granted by Google can cover events
across your accessible calendars. Expiracheck uses this permission
for its calendar integration, subject to your access rights for
the selected calendar.

## 3. Why the app uses this information

Expiracheck uses this information to:

- Store and display the items you manage.
- Calculate expiration dates and renewal periods.
- Schedule local expiration notifications.
- Identify your connected Google Account.
- Let you select a Google calendar.
- Create, update, and delete calendar events and reminders
  associated with items managed by the app.

Google user data is used to provide these features, not for
advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## 4. Storage and sharing

Expiracheck has no developer-operated backend server.
App data and authorization tokens are stored on your device.

When you use the calendar integration, relevant event information
is transmitted to Google and stored in Google Calendar.
Google processes that information under its own terms and
[Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Events are subject to the sharing settings of the selected calendar.
If you select a shared calendar, other people with access to that
calendar may be able to view or modify the events.

Expiracheck does not sell personal information or Google user data.
The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

If you contact the developer by email, the information you provide
is processed to respond to your inquiry. Please do not send passwords
or authorization tokens.

## 5. Google API Services Limited Use

Expiracheck's handling of information received through Google APIs
follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including its Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only for the disclosed calendar features.
It is not sold, transferred to advertising platforms or data brokers,
or used for advertising or credit decisions.

The developer does not routinely receive or review your calendar
data. If you voluntarily share specific information for support,
it is used to address your request.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or remove
the app's stored data using the options provided by your operating
system. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data,
although device backups may retain copies under your backup settings.

Calendar events remain in Google Calendar until you delete them.
Removing local app data, uninstalling Expiracheck, or revoking Google
access does not automatically delete existing calendar events.

You can edit or delete these events directly in Google Calendar.

You can revoke Expiracheck's access through your
[Google Account connections settings](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
Revoking access prevents further authorized calendar access until
you grant permission again.

The developer has no backend copy of your locally stored app data
or calendar events to delete on your behalf.

## 7. Security

Authorization tokens permit access to your Google data within the
permissions you grant. Keep your device protected and do not share
these tokens.

No application, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed
to be completely secure.

## 8. Website hosting

The Expiracheck website is hosted on GitHub Pages.
GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
For details, see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

This website hosting is separate from the mobile app's local storage
and Google Calendar integration.

correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to the processing

For information stored locally or in Google Calendar, use the
