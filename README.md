# Expiracheck

Expiracheck is a mobile application that helps you track expiration
dates, receive timely reminders, and renew items with a single tap.

## Features

- Manage items with expiration dates and renewal periods.
- View the time remaining before each item expires.
- Receive local notifications before expiration.
- Renew items with a single tap.
- Store and manage your items locally on your device.
- Connect Google Calendar to create and manage calendar events
  and reminders for your tracked items.

## Google Calendar integration

Expiracheck uses Google Account authorization to connect the app
to your Google calendars.

Google Account information identifies the connected account.
Access to your calendar list allows you to choose a calendar.
Calendar event access enables Expiracheck to create, update,
and delete events and their reminders for items managed by the app,
subject to your permissions for the selected calendar.

You retain control of your calendars and can edit or delete these
events directly in Google Calendar.

## Data and privacy

Expiracheck has no backend server. Item data and Google authorization
tokens are stored on your device. Calendar events and reminders
are stored in your Google Account through Google Calendar.

The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
Google user data is used to provide the Google Calendar integration.

For details about personal information and Google user data,
please read our [Privacy Policy](https://tomas-zivotsky.github.io/expiracheck-site/privacy-policy.html).
